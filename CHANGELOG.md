# Changelog

All notable changes to Spades are recorded here.
Versions follow Semantic Versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-09

### Added
- First release of Spades: A Brooklyn Rooftop Card Game for Windows, a native game written in Go.
- Full partnership Spades: bid your tricks (or Nil), spades are always trumps and can't be led until they're broken, 10 points per trick bid plus 1 per bag, set penalties, 100 off for every 10 bags, Nil worth 100 either way. Play to 500 or 300; a team that falls to −200 loses.
- Single player: you and a rooftop regular against two more, made up fresh for every game. Each has their own portrait, a name and title that fit their face (Nana Rose, Chef Vinny, Cap Eddie, DJ Kiki, Big Joey and many more) and their own way of talking.
- Play Together on the same computer: 2 to 4 people take turns, choose who partners whom, and get a "pass the computer" screen so nobody sees anyone else's cards. Rooftop regulars fill the empty seats.
- Play Together online: host a table or join a friend's by address, on your home network or over the internet. Everyone plays from their own seat, and if someone leaves, a rooftop regular takes over their cards.
- A hand-drawn Brooklyn deck: the Pizza Man, the Bodega Man (with his cat), the Jazzman and the Super for kings; the Singer, the Florist, the DJ and the Muralist for queens; the Delivery Kid, the Bagel Man, the Slugger and the Newsie for jacks. The Ace of Spades holds the skyline, and the card back has the bridge in gold.
- A deck-board table under string lights, with a cold soda and a slice, and a brick parapet along the top.
- Three original tunes: "Fire Escape Beat" (lo-fi hip-hop), "Stoop Soul" (organ and tenor sax) and "Bridge Lights" (late-night jazz piano), with volume control and mute.
- Sound effects for shuffling, dealing, bidding, going Nil, trumping, breaking spades, winning tricks, making or missing a contract, busting a Nil and collecting bags.
- Score box with each team's bid, tricks taken and bags, and every player's bid and tricks next to their name.
- Choice of pace: Easy, Regular or Quick.
- Saved record: games played and won, Nils made, and your best winning score.
- Credits & Changelog screen, How to Play guide, and the version number on the table and in the title bar.
