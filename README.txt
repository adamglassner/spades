SPADES - A Brooklyn Rooftop Card Game  (v@VERSION@)
An Ironite game. (c) 2026 Ironite.
====================================================

Open Spades from the Start menu (Ironite folder), your desktop, or the
Ironite Launcher. Or just double-click Spades.exe.

HOW TO PLAY
- Four players in two teams of two. Partners sit across from each other.
  All 52 cards are dealt, 13 each. Spades are always trumps.
- Everyone bids how many tricks they'll take. Partners' bids add up to
  the team's contract. Bid Nil to promise you'll take no tricks at all.
- Follow suit if you can; if you can't, play anything, including a
  spade. The highest spade wins, or else the highest card of the suit led.
- You can't lead a spade until spades are broken (unless you hold only
  spades).
- Make your contract: 10 points per trick bid, plus 1 per extra trick
  (a "bag"). Fall short: lose 10 per trick bid. Every 10 bags costs 100.
- Nil made: +100. Nil broken: -100.
- First team to 500 (or 300) wins. A team that drops to -200 loses.
- F2 = new game, M = music on/off, F11 = full screen.

WAYS TO PLAY
- Deal: you and a rooftop regular against two more, a new three every
  game.
- Play Together > Same Computer: 2 to 4 people share this PC. Pick who
  partners whom. Before each person's turn the game asks you to pass the
  computer over, and cards turn face down in between.
- Play Together > Online: one person picks Host, the others pick Join and
  type the host's address (shown on the host's screen). Empty seats are
  filled by rooftop regulars. Online games use port 47240; across the
  internet the host may need to forward that port on their router.

Your record and settings are saved in %APPDATA%\Ironite\Spades, outside
the install folder, so updating or reinstalling keeps them.

All card art, music and sounds are original and made by Ironite.
